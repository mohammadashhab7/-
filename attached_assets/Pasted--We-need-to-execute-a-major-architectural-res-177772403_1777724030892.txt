We need to execute a major architectural restructuring of the current system into a multi-division business platform.

STRICT RULES:
Do NOT rebuild the system from scratch.
Do NOT break any existing functionality (products, POS, orders, inventory, finance, dashboard).
Keep the current UI as much as possible.
Focus on refactoring logic, structure, and data separation.
All changes must be scalable, clean, and production-ready.

CORE OBJECTIVE:
Transform the system into 4 fully structured divisions:

1. Factory / Workshop
2. Showroom A
3. Showroom B
4. Showroom C

The system must support future expansion (adding more showrooms) without needing restructuring.

FACTORY (WORKSHOP):
The factory is a separate operational entity with different logic. It is responsible for:

* Raw materials inventory management
* Production processes
* Finished goods inventory
* Full financial system (income, expenses, profit)
* Selling products to:

  * Showrooms (internal clients)
  * External wholesale customers

CRITICAL BUSINESS RULE:
The relationship between Factory and Showrooms is DIRECT SALES, not stock transfer and not supply.
Every movement of goods from Factory to any Showroom must be treated as a sales transaction.
This means:

* The Factory creates a sales invoice
* The Showroom is treated as a customer
* The Factory records revenue
* The Showroom records an expense and stock increase

SHOWROOMS (3 BRANCHES):
Each showroom must behave as a fully independent business unit.

Each showroom must include:

1. Financial System:

* Revenue tracking
* Expense tracking
* Profit calculation
* Cash flow
* Employee salaries
* Daily and monthly reports

2. POS / Cashier System:

* Walk-in customer sales
* Invoice generation
* Payment handling
* Daily closing reports
* Order history

3. Inventory System:

* Tracks finished goods only
* Stock increases through purchases from Factory
* Stock decreases through sales
* Low stock alerts
* Stock valuation

4. Employee Management:

* Staff accounts per showroom
* Roles and permissions
* Optional attendance tracking and activity logs

RELATIONSHIP MODEL:
Factory to Showrooms:

* Showrooms are treated as clients
* Factory sells and showroom buys
* Every transaction generates an invoice (not internal transfer)

Showroom to Customers:

* Standard POS sales flow

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE REQUIREMENTS:

1. Multi-Division Separation:
   Each unit must have isolated data:

* Factory
* Showroom A
* Showroom B
* Showroom C

2. Central Admin Control:
   A super admin must be able to:

* View all divisions
* Access consolidated reports
* Manage system-wide settings

3. Role-Based Access:

* Each showroom user can only access their showroom data
* Factory users can only access factory data

4. Scalable Design:

* Ability to add unlimited showrooms later
* Use a structure such as business_unit_id or branch_id

5. Unified Product System:

* Products are defined once globally
* Shared across factory and showrooms
* No duplication

6. Financial Integrity:

* Every movement must have an accounting impact
* No silent stock transfers
* All transactions must be traceable

KEY FLOWS:

1. Factory Production Flow:
   Raw materials → Production → Finished goods

2. Factory Sale to Showroom:

* Factory creates invoice
* Showroom receives purchase
* Stock increases in showroom
* Expense recorded in showroom
* Revenue recorded in factory

3. Showroom Sale Flow:
   Stock → POS sale → Revenue

DATABASE AND LOGIC EXPECTATIONS:

* Introduce entities such as:

  * Business Units (Factory and Showrooms)
  * Transactions (Sales and Purchases)
  * Inventory per unit
* Maintain compatibility with existing data
* Migrate current data safely into the new structure

REPORTING REQUIREMENTS:

* Per showroom:

  * Sales
  * Expenses
  * Profit
* Factory:

  * Production cost
  * Sales
  * Profit
* Global:

  * Consolidated financial overview

DO NOT:

* Do NOT mix inventory between units
* Do NOT treat factory to showroom as internal transfer
* Do NOT duplicate products
* Do NOT break existing UI or workflows

EXPECTED OUTPUT:

* Clean modular architecture
* Updated database structure
* Clear financial flows
* Fully working POS, inventory, and finance per showroom
* Factory system integrated but logically separate
* System ready for real-world use and scaling
